# Montréal à double entente

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors comparing Montréal walking tours, plus people arranging private groups. French and English are the usual tour languages; Spanish is available for some dates. The interface supports French, English and Spanish.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors choose among collective 2-, 3- and 4-hour walks, private visits and specialized or digital formats, then contact Bernardo. Make clear that route, pace, language and depth adjust to the audience. Present Bernardo’s geographer profile, APGT credential and way of noticing the city.

## Brand Personality

Curious, perceptive, warm. Preserve the exact French name and bilingual signature: Secrets de l'Est, lanes from the West.

## Anti-references

Dense historical exposition, generic tourism brochures and decoration that obscures the offer. Tours Montréal supplied the page-flow reference; Bernardo's supplied photographs carry the identity.

## Design Principles

- Make the offer and practical information easy to read.
- Give the photographs and observations room.
- Let the placeholder mark lead the system: midnight navy and deep teal as the field, warm gold as the crossing/action colour, and blue, red, green and violet as restrained route cues.
- Retain the established editorial serif and legible sans pairing.
- Keep the signature fixed while translating useful interface copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard focus, alternative image descriptions and reduced motion. Use readable text, flexible layout and adequate contrast. No accessibility certification is claimed.

## Interactive prototype behaviour

- The homepage filters the collective and adapted offers by audience and reports the visible count.
- Each standard route opens a responsive detail dialog with duration, pace, language and a next-step CTA.
- The private-visit CTA opens a prototype request form. Submitted fields are stored only in the browser under `bernardo.prototype.request`; no message or booking is sent.
- The header menu collapses on small screens, the language order remains French, English, Español, and the current language is reflected in page links and dialog copy.
- Scroll progress, restrained reveals, header state and press feedback provide micro-interactions. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential movement.
